CHARACTER CONTROLLER

Pose, Motions & Rig

Use logical joints and named motions. Do not enter Blender bone names here. Motions use Saeed logical names such as rightUpperArm. Any real GLB bone can be controlled; recognized humanoid joints improve motion quality, but no fixed six-bone rig is required.

MOTION EDITOR

Custom Motions & Semantic Control

Create reusable motions from keyframes. Use logical joint names, not the GLB bone names. The brain calls semantic actions such as greet, think or nod.

UNIVERSAL RIG

Rig Mapping

Purpose: connect the bones in your GLB to Saeed logical joints. Automatic mapping is recommended first. Bone names may be PascalCase such as LeftUpperArm.

How to use Rig Mapping

Step 1: Load/select your GLB character. Step 2: Click Restore Automatic Mapping. Step 3: Check the 6 required joints below. Step 4: Only if automatic mapping is wrong, choose the exact real GLB bone from each dropdown and click Apply Manual Mapping.

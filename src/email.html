What Saeed can learn

IMAP can sync recent mail and inspect calendar (.ics) and contact (.vcf/.vcard) attachments. Detected calendar events are stored locally so Saeed can remind you on the event date.

Provider calendars/contacts themselves are not part of POP3/IMAP/SMTP. Full Gmail, Yahoo or Microsoft calendar/contact APIs require their provider authorization flow.
